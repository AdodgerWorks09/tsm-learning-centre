WHO WE ARE

A foundation for confident, independent learners.

TSM Learning Centre is a warm, inclusive setting for children aged 3 months through Primary 6. We combine the Ghana Education Service (GES) National Curriculum and Oxford International Curriculum with authentic Montessori methods.

Our goal is more than academic results. We nurture confident, independent, kind and happy children who love to learn and are prepared for every stage of their educational journey.

— Ms. Portia Arkutu
Principal, TSM Learning Centre

Three powerful approaches. One complete learning experience.

Balanced integration of GES and Oxford learning delivered through authentic Montessori practice.

GES National Curriculum

OUR PROGRAMMES

A pathway from first steps to Primary 6.

Each stage is designed around age-appropriate development, independence and joyful discovery.

01

Creche

3–18 months

Secure attachment, movement, communication, emotional wellbeing and sensory exploration.

  • 1:3 ratio
  • Key person system
  • Sensory play & music
02

Nursery 1

18 months–3 years

Practical life, sensorial activities, language, early numeracy and self-help skills.

  • Me, Family & Community
  • Body & Health
  • Environment & Living Things
03

Nursery 2

3–4 years

Language and literacy, numeracy, creative arts, living skills and understanding the world.

  • Phonics foundations
  • Early problem-solving
  • Physical development
04

Kindergarten

4–5 years

Consolidation of foundation skills and introduction to structured subject learning.

  • Reading readiness
  • Writing readiness
  • Mathematics readiness
05

Primary

Basic 1–Basic 6

English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies/Citizenship, ICT/Computing, Creative & Expressive Arts, French/Ghanaian Languages and more.

  • GES schemes + Oxford milestones
  • Dual assessment & reporting
  • Project-based learning & individual pacing

ADMISSIONS

Give your child roots to grow and wings to fly.

2

Submit form

3

Assessment / visit

4

Offer & accept
